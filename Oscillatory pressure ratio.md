- Oscillatory pressure ratio (OPR): Ratio of pressure swings at the distal and proximal ends of the endotracheal tube. **Read carefully: it is a ratio of delta pressure (peak to peak waveform at distal divided by at proximal end)
- An inverse relationship between compliance of the respiratory system (Crs) and OPR: 
	- When Crs increases, the attenuation of pressure swings increases and thus OPR decreases. OPR is minimal at the maximal Crs, at maximal lung recruitment and there is minimal overdistention. 
	- OPR is high both at low Paw, when the lungs are partly collapsed, and at high Paw, when the lungs are overdistended. 

Ref: Attenuation of pressure swings along the endotracheal tube is indicative of optimal distending pressure during high-frequency oscillatory ventilation in a model of acute lung injury. Pediatr Pulmonol. 2002 Jun;33(6):429-36.
