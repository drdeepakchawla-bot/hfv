- V=(C∆P)(1 — e<sup>-Ti /RC</sup>) ==This applies to any kind of ventilation==
- ∆P = PIP - PEEP
- RC = Time constant
- (1 — e<sup>-0.05 /0.1</sup>)=1-0.60=0.4
- (1 — e<sup>-0.05 /0.2</sup>)=1-0.78=0.22
- The above formula shows if the Ti is kept the same, an increase in the time constant leads to less delivery of tidal volume (It seems right if the time constant increases because of increased resistance. It may seem counterintuitive that an increase in compliance will also lead to a lower value of (1 — e<sup>-Ti /RC</sup>), but remember that compliance also figures in the initial part of the equation where it is increasing the TV
- Why there is an exponential function in the above equation - because as you see below in the diagram, there is a plateau. Once Ti is 'sufficient', no further increase in the tidal volume

Therefore in HFOV, the TV delivery depends on the following:
1. Increase in amplitude leads to a linear increase in tidal volume as the delta P increases![[Screen Shot 2023-02-09 at 8.56.23 PM.png]]
2. Increase in frequency> if I:E ratio remains the same, the total time for one oscillatory cycle decreases>Ti decreases>Ti/TC ratio decreases and tidal volume decreases. The relationship is not linear![[Screen Shot 2023-02-09 at 8.56.34 PM.png]]
3. I:E ratio: If the I:E ratio is increased from 1:2 to 1:1, total inspiratory time increases. Therefore the tidal volume will increase. The relationship is not linear. Also, the amount of increase depends on the following:
	1. At higher frequencies the relative increase in TV is more as oscillatory cycles are shorter. At lower frequencies, already Ti is longer and therefore further increase may have less effect. ![[Screen Shot 2023-02-09 at 9.05.33 PM.png]]
	2. Lung mechanics - if ventilation is going on at a steeper part of the PV curve, more increase in TV than if on less steep parts
	3. Lung mechanics - if the time constant of the lung is longer (e.g., increased resistance)-more effect of an increase in TV. If the time constant is shorter (e.g., HMD)- less effect of an increase in TV
