Rohrer constants are coefficients of linear and nonlinear resistance, K1 and K2 respectively, that describe the phenomenon of turbulent flow in airways. They are used to measure the inspiratory resistance of endotracheal tubes (ETT), which are tubes inserted into the trachea to provide artificial ventilation⁶. Rohrer's equation for airway resistance is:

```
Pres = K1 * V + K2 * V^2
```

where Pres is pressure drop, V is flow rate, and K1 and K2 are Rohrer constants.
