| Brand         | Mechanism of generating HFV                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sensor Medics | Piston oscillator (large diaphragm)                                                                                                                             |
| VN500/600     | Diaphragm oscillator                                                                                                                                            |
| Fabian        | Diaphragm oscillator                                                                                                                                            |
| SLE 5000/6000 | Reverse jet oscillator (valve-less mechanism involving forward and backward-facing jets that alternately direct gas into the patient and facilitate exhalation) |
| Sophie        | Reciprocating valve oscillator (using a valve alternately directs gas flow into the patient circuit and away from the patient to generate active exhalation)    |
|               |                                                                                                                                                                 |
