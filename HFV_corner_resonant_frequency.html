Frequency response of the lung drag the f slider — the white dot moves

|Z| (blue) is the pressure cost per unit oscillatory flow. It falls steeply through the corner frequency fc, bottoms out at the resonant frequency f0 (where elastic and inertive reactance cancel, so |Z| = R), then rises again. Transmission to the alveoli (purple) is highest at low frequency and at resonance.

Lung vibration — alveolar excursion

Amplitude of the alveolar "wiggle" ∝ tidal volume delivered at this frequency. Raise f and the excursion shrinks — the basis of tiny-VT HFOV.

Pressure transmission

Airway-opening amplitude is fixed; alveolar amplitude = T(f) × ΔPaw. When the lung is under-damped (low R), the alveolar bar can match or exceed the airway bar near resonance — over-transmission.

The math, with your current values substituted

Series respiratory impedance: Z(f) = R + j·(2πf·I − 1/(2πf·C))  →  |Z| = √( R² + (2πfI − 1/(2πfC))² )

How to read this clinically

Model: linear series R–I–C respiratory impedance. fc = 1/(2πRC), f0 = 1/(2π√(IC)); at resonance |Z| = R (minimum). Normalised alveolar excursion equals the pressure-transmission ratio because alveolar volume = C × alveolar pressure.
