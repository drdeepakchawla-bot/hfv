HFV Tidal Volume Simulator

VT = (C · ΔP) · (1 − e−Ti/RC)

In conventional ventilation Ti spans many time constants, so the equilibration term ≈ 1 and VT ≈ C·ΔP. In HFV the inspiratory time is a fraction of one time constant (τ = RC), so the term collapses and itself controls delivered volume. Drag the sliders and watch where the term bites.
