1 · Pressure dampening along the airway how the wave travels

The set amplitude is not transmitted fully to the alveoli — impedance damps each oscillation as it travels distally. Watch the same breath at three sites. Note that low tidal volume does not guarantee low alveolar pressure swing.

2 · Impedance vs frequency — finding the efficient window fc & f₀

Total respiratory impedance |Z| = √(R² + (2πf·I − 1/2πf·C)²). It is dominated by elastance at low f and by inertance at high f, with a broad minimum at the resonant frequency. The shaded band from fc to f₀ is the low-pressure-cost operating window where oscillations are transmitted most efficiently.

3 · Alveolar pressure transmission & the OPR barotrauma

Fraction of the airway-opening swing that reaches the alveoli, across frequency. Transmission is highest at very low frequency and peaks again at resonance (where alveolar swing can equal or exceed the airway-opening swing). Raising frequency increases dampening — the core reason HFV protects the lung.
