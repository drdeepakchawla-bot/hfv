- Inspiration and expiration are both active. So proximal airway pressure is negative during expiration. 
1. **MAP**: A controlled leak or low-pass filter (see [[Brands of HFV ventilators]] for diagram) allows exhaled+excess fresh gas to exit the system without dampening the high-frequency pressure swings. The balance between the inflow of fresh gas and the controlled leak determines the MAP.
2. **Amplitude**: The amplitude of pressure oscillations is determined by power applied to the piston (SensorMedics terminology)>>>determines the tidal volume that is delivered to the lungs
3. **Frequency**: The frequency at which the piston moves to and fro (or the corresponding mechanism in other ventilator brands). The range is 3 to 20 Hz - which varies with the brands.
4. **Percentage inspiratory time**: 33% to 50% of one respiratory cycle
5. **Sigh breaths**: Can be superimposed or interposed with HFV (spontaneous sigh breaths also perform the same function)
6. **Volume guarantee**: Many HFOV machines can measure tidal volume and this can be used to 'servo' control the amplitude changes that happen with the change in compliance. Values range from 0.8 to 2.0 ml/kg. Starting value should be 1.6-1.8 ml/kg.
